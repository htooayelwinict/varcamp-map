# Aries and Capricorn architecture

Whole-building designs: copper spiral galleries (Aries) and asymmetric mountain and coastal citadel (Capricorn). Generated with OpenAI built-in image generation using the approved Leo and Cancer sprites as rendering references. The rejected badge-on-house and Renaissance previews are excluded.

Masters and prompts: `art/source/horned-citadels/`. Compiler: `tools/art/horned-citadels.ts`. Published sprites: `assets/tilesets/original/{aries,capricorn}-citadel.png`. All sprites are fitted uniformly by nearest-neighbor sampling into 384px canvases; no zodiac plaques are added by the compiler.
