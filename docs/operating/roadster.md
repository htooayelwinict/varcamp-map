# Stationary Roadster display

The arrival plaza contains a silver angular Roadster-inspired car on a fixed landing pad. It rests, ignites white/cyan jets, rises, hovers, makes one full flip, descends and lands. Its existing native 12.8-second animation and artwork are unchanged.

The animation is at tile (83,53), in a 5 by 5 tile frame. The fixed display bay now has a solid 5 by 5 tile footprint at (83,53), covering its full hover and flip motion, so Wokas cannot walk through the display or appear to stand on its roof. The surrounding walkway remains open. `world/roadster-physics.json` and `tools/maps/roadster-physics.ts` record this exact collision-only successor. The bay reuses the existing terrain tile beneath the plaza atmosphere with a solid mask, preserving its appearance without new artwork or a new atlas. The public guide paths do not cross this footprint.

`art/source/roadster-poses.png` is the original generated image (SHA-256 `08b8e46661d57bb4f44ea0e09bdee91debc3311440d1d2406a949e164bd86cd0`). It is actually 1254×1254, despite the generation prompt requesting 1024×1024. The full prompt and authoring parameters are retained in `roadster-authoring.json`. Private photographs were visual references only and are not distributed. No sponsor logo is baked into the display. Cold-gas jets are stylized animation, not a technical simulation of a real vehicle.

## v1 pose compile

The compiler divides the source at 0,313,627,940,1254, finds each pose's alpha bounds, applies one 0.4 nearest-neighbor scale, thresholds alpha to 0/255 and centers it at integer coordinates over the fixed pad. `art/source/roadster.json` records each source crop, target rectangle, timing and exact inputs. The v1 cycle is sixteen whole-pose frames: landed 3s, ignition 0.6s, rise 0.5s, low hover 0.7s, hover 1.2s, jump 0.5s, six roll poses 0.35s each, recovery 0.5s, descend 0.8s, touchdown 0.6s and rest 2.3s.

## v2 compiled motion

`art/source/roadster-v2-authoring.json` keeps the 100 ms cadence and 12.8 s cycle (landed 3.0s, ignition 0.6s, rise 0.5s, low hover 0.7s, hover 1.3s, jump 0.5s, turn 1.8s, recovery 0.6s, descend 0.9s, touchdown 0.6s, rest 2.3s). The turn and recovery use the upright jump sprite instead of the six generated roll viewpoints. Every car pixel still comes from the v1 crop recipe; the compiler synthesises 128 steps, merges consecutive identical steps (86 frames) and packs them with the same 32px carrier scheme. Per-frame receipts (`frames[]` in the metadata: phase, pose, vertical offset, rotation angle, glow intensity and placed glow pixels, shadow level and half-width, pad light state, dust frame, light levels) make every effect testable.

- **Smooth motion.** Each phase declares its motion: `ground` (fixed), `tween` (the drawing enters at the previous phase's final roof height and eases in/out to its own placement, optionally `endDy` px lower), `roll` (the fixed-pivot turn and upright recovery described below) or `drop` (ease-in fall to the pad, then a 2 px / 1 px settle). Low hover and hover add a ±2 px sinusoidal bob (1 s period) once their tween has settled; the bob offset carries into the next tween so nothing snaps. The first and last frames are the grounded v1 poses at their exact v1 anchor.
- **Thrusters.** Plumes and puffs are located in each pose by colour class (pale cyan seeds grown two pixels into their white cores; blobs of at least 12 pixels, 6 seeds and 6 rows). Beneath each one the compiler adds a cyan/white bloom: ordered-dithered 255-alpha pixels over the transparent terrace, additive light on the pad and on the plume pixels themselves. Intensity cycles through three variants (0.85/1.0/1.15) every 100 ms while airborne, ramps up at ignition, and is brightest (1.3×) at ignition, jump and touchdown. Additional bloom is disabled during the turn and upright recovery.
- **Ground effects.** A soft elliptical shadow darkens the pad under the car; it narrows from a 54 px to a 30 px half-width and fades over four altitude levels. While airborne the pad's centre bar carries two chasing cyan segments (200 ms stride) and the end strips alternate; ignition blinks the strips, touchdown flashes bar and strips, grounded holds leave the pad static. Ignition and touchdown each emit a three-frame expanding ring of pale dust/ion dots at pad level.
- **Lights.** The thin white front strip (bright pixels in the lower-left of an upright body) pulses softly through three levels on a 1.6 s period. The red rear bar flares at ignition and touchdown (hot core plus a two-pixel additive halo for 300 ms, then a one-pixel halo) and brightens through the descent.
- **One full flip.** The turn uses only the upright jump sprite (pose 5), sampled with nearest-neighbour rotation around the fixed frame pivot `(80,74)` at unchanged scale. Sixteen equal 22.5° advances complete exactly one 360° turn in 1.6 s, then the same sprite holds upright through recovery before descent. Its existing jet pixels rotate with it; additional stationary bloom is off during the turn and recovery, then resumes on descent. No camera-view swaps or checkerboard ghost drawings are used. `rotationDeg` in each frame receipt records the actual transform; it is `null` outside the flip/recovery interval.

Binary alpha is preserved everywhere: effects on transparent terrace are dithered 255-alpha pixels, effects on the pad or the car are colour changes only, and every visible pixel stays inside x < 144 and off the frame edges.
