# Visiting VarCamp

Save the plaza URL supplied by WorkAdventure for the sponsored venue in your bookmarks. The development devbox is not the venue host. Choose a name of at most 10 characters and an avatar; WorkAdventure rejects longer names. If the "Name is too long" error remains after you shorten the name, reload the page. Keep camera and microphone off, and enter at the central plaza. Walk with arrow keys or WASD. The welcome directory and Quest Journal provide destination buttons; ordinary doors remain available. Ask the help desk for the current programme. No published schedule is embedded in this world.

The twelve numbered speaker buildings surround the compact welcome court. Their interiors are separate maps. Solar Hall and Lunar Hall are north-west and north-east; the maze gate is north, Festival Park is west, Star Harbor is east, and Exploration Court is south. Reception has a large colourful VarCamp floor logo, partner panels, a directory and Lyra the guide. Directly south of the court, the expo plaza holds ten booths in two rows facing a shared aisle (sponsor booths on blue carpet, community hubs on wood) with the guide corridors left open between them. To talk to a guide, open the avatar menu at the top right, choose **All settings**, then choose **Talk to Nearby Guides** from the menu list. Follow gold arrows and framed signs; use the signed plaza return in each destination.

Festival Park branches to the Nebula Arcade and Orbit Playground. Star Harbor branches to Constellation Shore and the large FA SHIP. Exploration Court branches to the four elemental workshop districts, Star Garden, Moon Café, Moon Grotto and Stargazer Terrace. Every one is a separate map. Nebula Arcade has six cabinets for War Brokers, DEADSHOT and HaxBall. Stand on a cabinet’s front mat and press Space to open its official game in a new tab. The park has flower gardens, a pond walk, trees and picnic seating. Orbit Playground has a timed Woka course: START → Space → pads 1–7 → FINISH. The clock records your own time and private personal best. See the [park and arcade guide](park-arcade.md). The journal lists all public destinations, with twelve speaker rooms first.

Exploration is optional. Explicitly activate zodiac discovery prompts inside the twelve speaker rooms. Collect one sigil in each elemental district, then visit the telescope on Stargazer Terrace; its four-sigil gate leads to Hidden Observatory. Explicitly activate the observatory discovery shrine to earn Stargazer. Orbit Maze and Comet Maze have their own paths, hints and explicit reward prompts. No quest is required to attend a session, use the directory or leave a room.

Open Quest Journal to see confirmed, pending and undiscovered stamps. A failed save stays undiscovered and gives a retry action. Use one active quest tab per visitor. Flags are private and use the `varcamp2026` world scope; guest progress is browser-local. Keep the same browser profile and site data. Private browsing, another device or cleared storage may not carry guest progress. An accepted API write is not proof of server durability. Actual cross-map/two-independent-visitor persistence still needs the hosting rehearsal; do not promise account-wide or device-wide progress on the strength of map tests.

Presentation and staff areas exist in native map metadata, but their real staff-role/server enforcement, microphones, cameras, screen sharing and direct/relayed media require the separate hosted checks. Camera/microphone defaults do not demonstrate device permission behavior or a successful call. Follow the organiser's actual programme and media instructions when these are ready.
